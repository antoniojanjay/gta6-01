Sunset Streets: Solara Bay — a 2D top-down open-world crime game: explore an original sun-soaked city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the police under a 1–5 star wanted level, and finish six story missions to clear Leo Marlow's debt.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Controls: WASD move/drive, E steal or exit a car, F punch, click to shoot, P pause, M mute, N minimap zoom. Progress saves automatically in the browser.
